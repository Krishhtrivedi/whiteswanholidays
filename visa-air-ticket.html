Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about visas, documentation, and flight reservations.

How early should I apply for an international visa?

For eVisa countries like UAE, Thailand, or Vietnam, 1 to 2 weeks in advance is typically sufficient. For sticker visas (Schengen, UK, USA, Canada, Japan), we strongly recommend initiating paperwork 6 to 12 weeks before departure due to seasonal appointment waiting times.

Does White Swan Holidays guarantee visa approval?

No agency can guarantee visa approval, as it rests solely with consular officers. What we guarantee is a 100% meticulous, error-free application aligned with the embassy's exact requirements to maximise your approval probability.

Can you arrange flight reservations for visa applications?

Yes. Many embassies require a verified round-trip flight itinerary before issuing a visa. We issue genuine itinerary reservations suitable for visa submissions so you do not risk buying non-refundable tickets before approval.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my flight?

Our ticketing team provides direct post-booking support. Rather than waiting on hold with call centers, simply message us, and we handle the rescheduling, date changes, or airline refund process on your behalf according to fare rules.
