Understanding MICE

What is MICE Travel?

MICE is a specialised sector of business tourism dedicated to planning, booking, and executing impactful corporate group events. We bring structure, elegance, and peace of mind to each facet:

M
Corporate Conclaves

Meetings

From executive board retreats and quarterly business reviews to strategic workshops. We secure private meeting spaces with high-speed internet, premium AV tech, and attentive business dining.

  • Leadership offsites & board meetings
  • Departmental strategy workshops
  • High-spec AV, projection & videoconferencing
  • Discreet, distraction-free boardrooms
I
Reward & Recognition

Incentives

Rewarding top sales performers, valued dealers, and high-achieving teams with memorable travel experiences. We curate luxury resort stays, bespoke gala nights, and unique cultural explorations.

  • Dealer, distributor & star-performer tours
  • Exclusive gala dinners & award ceremonies
  • Catamaran cruises, desert safaris & team dinners
  • Branded welcome kits & executive gifting
C
Large-Scale Gatherings

Conferences

Annual company assemblies, regional sales summits, product launches, and industry conventions. We handle massive hall layouts, stage production, acoustics, breakout rooms, and delegate transit.

  • Keynote stage setups, LED walls & lighting
  • Delegate registration & badge printing desks
  • Parallel breakout sessions & banquet catering
  • Simultaneous multi-city delegate arrivals
E
Trade & Expos

Exhibitions

Facilitating participation in premier domestic and international industrial trade fairs, expos, and summits. We coordinate group flight tickets, hotels near expo venues, and daily shuttles.

  • Trade fair delegate group travel & stays
  • Hotels in close proximity to convention halls
  • Daily scheduled transfers & booth staff support
  • Overseas visa processing & invitations
