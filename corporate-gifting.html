Craftsmanship & Finishes

Materials We Work With

We combine artisan skill with precision computer-guided cutting, laser engraving, and UV printing across a diverse spectrum of raw materials.

Natural Grain

Wood

Premium teak, walnut, beechwood, and pine with rich natural grains, deep laser engraving, and satin or gloss lacquer finishes for timeless warmth.

Modern Precision

Metal

Anodized aluminium, brushed stainless steel, and alloy castings with fine chamfered edges, rotary engraving, and scratch-resistant matte coatings.

Clear Elegance

Glass

Toughened float glass, beveled plaques, and frosted glass with high-definition multi-color UV printing and elegant wooden or metallic bases.

Pure Brilliance

Crystal

Flawless K9 optical crystal with 3D internal laser etching, hand-polished facet cuts, and exceptional light refraction for top-tier executive awards.

Versatile & Vibrant

Acrylic

Ultra-clear cast acrylic, smoked tints, and sandwich frames featuring flame-polished edges and laser cutting for contemporary recognition displays.

Regal Heritage

Brass

Hand-beaten and cast brass with antique oxidized or 24k gold-tone electroplating, intricate chemical etching, and clear protective lacquer coats.

Earth-Conscious

Eco-friendly Materials

Natural cork, fast-growing bamboo, recycled wheat straw composites, organic cotton, jute, and plantable seed paper for sustainable brand messaging.
