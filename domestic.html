Season by Season Guide

Best time to visit different regions

India's subcontinental geography ensures there is an ideal region to explore every single month. Here is when to visit each part of the country.

North India & Himalayas

Summer & Snow
April – October & Dec – Feb

Kashmir, Himachal, Ladakh, Uttarakhand:

  • April to June: Pleasant summer escape, blooming tulip gardens in Srinagar, clear skies in Shimla and Manali.
  • June to September: Prime season for Leh & Ladakh high-altitude mountain passes and Pangong Lake.
  • December to February: Magical snow, skiing in Gulmarg and Auli, and winter wonderland landscapes.

West India & Deserts

Winter Royalty
October – March

Rajasthan, Gujarat, Diu, Daman, Mahabaleshwar:

  • October to March: Crisp winter days and pleasant evenings ideal for Jaipur, Udaipur palaces and Jaisalmer desert camps.
  • November to February: Celebrated Rann Utsav in Kutch, temple circuits in Somnath & Dwarka, and strawberry harvest in Mahabaleshwar.

South India & Backwaters

Tropical Bliss
September – March & Jun – Aug

Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh:

  • September to March: Cool, sunny weather for Alleppey houseboats, Munnar tea hills, and Tirupati & Madurai temple darshans.
  • June to August: The famous Kerala monsoon, considered the traditional peak season for Ayurvedic wellness and rejuvenation therapies.

Goa & Coastal Escapes

Sun & Sea
November – February

North & South Goa, Diu, Daman, Coastal Konkan:

  • November to February: Balmy coastal breezes, calm sea conditions for water sports, beach shacks, and lively festive vibe.
  • Monsoon (July to September): Quiet, emerald-green Goa with lush paddy fields, gushing Dudhsagar waterfalls, and peaceful boutique stays.

East & Northeast India

Crystal Views
October – May

Sikkim, Darjeeling, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh:

  • October to December: Crystal-clear mountain skies with dramatic Kanchenjunga panoramas and crisp Himalayan mornings.
  • March to May: Spring bloom across high valleys with wild rhododendrons and pleasant sightseeing weather in Shillong & Gangtok.

Central India & Wildlife

Safari Season
October – June

Madhya Pradesh, Corbett & Ranthambore:

  • October to June: Tiger safari gates remain open in Bandhavgarh, Kanha, Pench, and Corbett.
  • November to February: Mild temperatures for historical circuits like Ujjain Mahakal, Khajuraho temples, and Bhedaghat marble rocks.
