Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from international patients and accompanying family members.

How does White Swan Holidays assist medical travellers?

We take complete charge of non-medical travel logistics. This includes finding vetted accommodations near your hospital, arranging airport meet-and-greets, booking daily cabs, coordinating language translators, and assisting with visa paperwork.

Can you arrange serviced apartments with kitchen facilities?

Yes. Many patients require specific diets during recuperation. We offer fully equipped serviced apartments and apart-hotels with private kitchens, refrigerators, and sanitised cooking facilities in all major medical cities.

Do you provide wheelchair or patient-accessible transport?

Yes. Upon request, we arrange spacious MPVs, vans, or vehicles equipped for wheelchair access, alongside trained drivers who assist gently with boarding and luggage at both the airport and hospital.

What if our hospital stay gets extended?

Medical treatments can sometimes require extra recovery days. Our local team coordinates flexible room extensions with hotels and modifies flight return tickets with minimum airline amendment charges.

How does Indian Medical Visa (MED) assistance work?

Once you select your treating hospital and obtain a formal medical invitation letter, we guide you through the online e-Medical Visa application, document checklists, and flight booking confirmations required by Indian immigration.

Do you offer excursions for accompanying family members?

Yes. While the patient rests, visiting relatives can take stress-free, private city tours, visit local shopping markets, or take spiritual day trips, with schedules completely adaptable to the patient's schedule.
